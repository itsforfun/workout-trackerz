Modifications:
- Mapped all 203 public graph structures: 119 direct, 51 compound, and 33 contextual.
- Selected exact source elements from the verified BodyParts3D 4.0 IS-A and PART-OF archives.
- Combined direct meshes into eight region GLBs and packaged compound structures as lazy region supplements.
- Applied a reviewed 18% vertex-removal web LOD to shoulder meshes so base plus first supplement stays inside the cumulative region budget.
- Converted Z-up millimetres to Y-up metres, indexed duplicate vertices, and added application grouping and category materials.
- Enforced one mesh owner per source geometry; aggregate and alias structures reference the canonical owner as context.
- Context entries reference related source-backed meshes only; no missing joint, ligament, muscle, or tendon geometry was inferred.

Odyr's use (models/body.glb, built by tools/build-body.py from the Somakine web pack above):
- Merged the muscles into Odyr's 14 muscle groups plus "other muscles", and all bones into one skeleton mesh.
- Simplified each mesh to a triangle budget (about 4,500 per group, 16,000 for the skeleton), centred the body.
- BodyParts3D has no rectus abdominis or latissimus dorsi; the 3D body shows abs through the abdominal wall
  (external oblique) and the upper back through the rhomboids, teres and infraspinatus.
